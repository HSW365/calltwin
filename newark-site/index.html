Questions

Straight answers.

Is someone really answering at 2 a.m.?

Yes. Every call is answered by New Ark's AI receptionist, which takes your details, walks you through emergency steps like shutting off your water, and puts the job on dispatch's board right away. The office calls you back to confirm.

Do you charge for estimates?

On-site assessments for new installs are free. The online estimate gives a typical range; your firm price is set after we see the job.

Can one company handle plumbing, HVAC, geothermal and sprinklers?

That's the point of New Ark. One contractor coordinates all four, so a boiler swap, a new water line and a sprinkler tie-in happen on one schedule.

How do I pay?

Zelle, Cash App or card through Stripe. Use the Pay Invoice section, then send the payment notice so we can match it to your invoice.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, on new systems such as furnaces, AC, water heaters and geothermal loops. Ask during your assessment.

What area do you cover?

Northern and Western New Jersey, based out of Phillipsburg. If you're close to the line, call and we'll confirm.
