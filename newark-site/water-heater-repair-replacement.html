  • Rumbling, popping or rusty water
  • Full replacement and haul-away
  • Expansion tanks and code updates

What to do right now

  1. If the tank is leaking, turn off the cold water supply valve on top of the heater.
  2. For a gas heater, turn the gas control to OFF or PILOT. For electric, switch off its breaker.
  3. Note the brand and age from the label on the tank if you can. It helps us bring the right parts.
  4. Call 908-454-4043.
