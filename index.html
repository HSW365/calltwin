Questions business owners ask.

Can I keep my existing business number?

Yes. The implementation can use call forwarding/routing from the business's existing number to the configured CallTwin voice endpoint, depending on the carrier and telephony provider.

Does CallTwin require Twilio?

No. The product is designed to keep the telephony layer provider-neutral. The live calling provider must still be configured before production calls can be answered.

What happens after the $500 setup?

After payment, the business completes onboarding. HSW365 configures the business knowledge, AI behavior, call workflow and voice connection, then tests the experience before launch.

Can it book appointments?

Yes, when the required calendar or scheduling integration is connected and the business provides its booking rules.
