Before we get there

  • Water running: shut the main valve where the line comes into the house.
  • Gas smell: leave now, don't touch switches, call 911 and your utility.
  • No heat: check the thermostat, the breaker and the furnace switch, then call.
  • Sprinkler going off: close the system control valve if it's safe, then call.
Is someone really answering at 2 a.m.?

Yes. Marcus, our AI front desk, answers every call. He takes your details, walks you through emergency steps, and texts the job to the owner right away. The owner calls you back to confirm.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Assessments for new installs and replacements are free, and you can request a written estimate online with photos. Repairs are priced once we've seen the problem.

How does the online estimate work?

Send photos and a description. The owner prices every line himself and sends you a proposal by text or email. You can ask questions, request changes, or sign and pay a deposit online.

Can one company really do plumbing, HVAC, geothermal and sprinklers?

That's why New Ark exists. One contractor coordinates all four, so the work happens on one schedule without three companies pointing at each other.

How do I pay?

Zelle, Cash App or card. Use the Pay a bill section, then send the payment notice so we can match it to your invoice.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, on new systems such as furnaces, central air, water heaters and geothermal. Ask during your assessment.

What area do you cover?

Northern and Western New Jersey, from our office in Phillipsburg. If you're close to the edge, call and we'll confirm.
