Questions

Do you design fire sprinkler systems or only install them?

New Ark designs and installs fire sprinkler systems for residential and commercial buildings, and provides repairs and regular inspections.

Is New Ark a licensed fire sprinkler contractor?

Yes. New Ark holds New Jersey Fire Sprinkler Contractor License #P01631, alongside its Master Plumber and HVAC Contractor licenses.

Why use one contractor for plumbing, HVAC and fire protection?

Water, heat, air and fire lines share the same walls, ceilings and schedule. One contractor keeps them coordinated on new construction and renovations.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. New Ark serves commercial and residential customers, including public and private sector clients.

A sprinkler head is leaking. What should I do?

If there is any sign of fire or smoke, get out and call 911. If it is only a leak, shut off the sprinkler supply valve if you know where it is, then message us here. Calls are answered 24/7.

How do I get an estimate?

message us here, message us, or request a free written estimate online. Tell us the building type and whether it is new construction or a renovation.

Fire sprinkler systems designed, installed and inspected

New Ark is a New Jersey licensed fire sprinkler contractor (License #P01631). We design and install fire sprinkler systems in homes, offices, shops and other commercial buildings, and we handle repairs and regular inspections on the systems we work on. Because New Ark is also a licensed master plumber and HVAC contractor, one company can plan the sprinkler piping around the plumbing, ductwork and heating on the same job.

New construction

Sprinkler design and installation coordinated with the plumbing and HVAC rough-in, so trades are not working around each other.

Renovations and additions

Adding or extending sprinkler coverage when a building is remodeled, expanded or changes use.

Repairs

Leaking heads, damaged piping and valves. If a head is leaking, shut off the sprinkler supply valve if you know where it is and message us here.

Inspections

Regular inspections so building owners and managers know the system is ready when it is needed.

Winter tip: sprinkler piping in unheated areas can freeze. See our guide to keeping fire sprinkler pipes from freezing. For more on New Ark's fire protection work, visit New Ark Fire Protection Services.

If there is fire or smoke, get everyone out and call 911 first.
