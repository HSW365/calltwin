Geothermal systems

We install and maintain geothermal heating and cooling systems. These systems pump energy from the earth to heat and cool buildings. They are environmentally friendly, cost-efficient and use clean energy instead of fossil fuels. Our work includes new construction for auxiliary heating and duct systems, and the add-on or retrofitting of furnace and duct systems.

About New Ark.

New Ark Plumbing, Heating & Air Conditioning LLC provides high quality plumbing, HVAC and fire protection services to both public and private sector clients.

During his long career, with over 40 years in the business, the company's owner has made it his personal mission to put client satisfaction first, often telling his clients that the firm's intent is to make the company's name synonymous with "reliability."

We stay abreast of changes in the technology of the trade by participating in industry training programs and seminars, and have added geothermal system installation and maintenance to our list of services. Our mission: always be on time, on budget and on spec.

Before we get there

  • Water running: shut the main valve where the line comes into the house.
  • Gas smell: leave now, don't touch switches, call 911 and your utility.
  • No heat: check the thermostat, the breaker and the furnace switch, then call.
  • Sprinkler going off: close the system control valve if it is safe, then call.
