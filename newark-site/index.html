Questions

Straight answers.

Is someone really answering at 2 a.m.?

Yes. Every call is answered by New Ark's AI receptionist. It takes your details, walks you through emergency steps like shutting off your water, and texts the job to the owner right away. The owner calls you back to confirm.

Do you charge for estimates?

On-site assessments for new installs and replacements are free. Your firm price is set after we see the job.

Can one company handle plumbing, HVAC, geothermal and sprinklers?

That's the point of New Ark. One contractor coordinates all four, so the work happens on one schedule without three companies pointing at each other.

How do I pay?

Zelle, Cash App or card through Stripe. Use the Pay section above, then send the payment notice so we can match it to your invoice.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, on new systems such as furnaces, AC, water heaters and geothermal. Ask during your assessment.

What area do you cover?

Northern and Western New Jersey, based out of Phillipsburg. If you're close to the line, call and we'll confirm.

Emergency first steps

While you wait

  • Water: shut the main valve, usually where the line enters the house.
  • Gas smell: leave now, don't flip switches, call 911 and your utility.
  • No heat: check the thermostat, breaker and furnace switch, then call.
  • Sprinkler going off: close the system control valve if safe, then call.
