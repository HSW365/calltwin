The rule we never break

The AI never writes a price

Most tools let an AI guess at numbers and call it an estimate. That is how you lose money or lose the job. Here, the AI writes the words. Every dollar comes from your own past prices or your own typing, and anything new is flagged yellow until you price it. Totals are worked out to the penny, never guessed.

Questions

Straight answers

What is a conversion?

One conversion is one job turned into a finished estimate: your photos, video and voice note go in, and a drafted estimate with heading, scope and line items comes out. Editing it, saving it, making the PDF and emailing it are all free.

Does the AI set my prices?

Never. The AI writes the words only. Prices come from your own past lines (your price book) or from you typing them. A new line with no price yet is highlighted yellow so you cannot miss it, and every total is calculated to the penny.

What trades does it work for?

Any contractor who prices by the job: plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, remodeling, painting, landscaping, fire protection and more. It reads what is in your photos and what you say, so it adapts to your trade.

How long does it take?

About a minute from the photos to a drafted estimate, then as long as you need to set your prices. Most small jobs go from the driveway to the customer's inbox in a few minutes.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. You sign in with an 8 digit PIN, take photos on your phone, and talk instead of typing. If you can send a text message, you can use it.

What does it cost to try?

Nothing. Every new account gets 3 free estimates with no card. After that, plans start at $49 a month for jobs under $5K, or you can buy a pack of pay-per-estimate credits with no subscription.

What if the AI cannot read my job?

If the AI returns an error or cannot make sense of the photos, you are not charged. The credit goes straight back to your account and you can try again with a clearer photo or a few typed words.

Can I put my own logo and colors on the PDF?

You get your business name, address, phone, licenses and brand color on every document. Crew, Pro and Commercial plans remove the small HSW365 AI Estimate line from the footer; Solo and pay-per-estimate documents keep it.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from the billing page. Your saved estimates stay in your account. Pay-per-estimate packs never expire.
