What to do right now

  1. Shut off the main water valve if water is spreading. It's usually where the water line enters the house, often in the basement.
  2. If water is near outlets, electrical panels or appliances, stay clear and turn off power at the breaker only if you can do it safely and dry.
  3. If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 first, then call us.
  4. Call 908-454-4043. Tell us what's leaking, where, and your address.
